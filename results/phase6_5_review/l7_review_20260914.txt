Phase 6.5 L7 zero-fit review package

Scope:
1. influence/branch_anisotropy.py
2. probe_branch_anisotropy.py
3. verify_branch_anisotropy.py

Feature contract:
- exact shell-2 nodes allocate unit mass equally over every adjacent hop-1 parent;
  no BFS discovery order chooses a branch.
- branch totals are normalised by shell-2 size.  The published features are
  maximum share and natural-log entropy.  Empty shells retain two zeros and a
  flag.
- feature extraction reads only graph adjacency and node identity.

Residual contract:
- primary: reported rf_log1p OOF betweenness, structural radius 3, all seeds 0..9.
- sensitivities: historical raw betweenness and raw spread_mean, independently
  labelled.  Neither can replace a missing primary arm.
- adjusted residual tails use ascending np.lexsort((node, adjusted_delta)); the
  first k nodes are over-predicted and the last k are under-predicted, with
  k=max(10, round(0.05*n)).
- each feature reports directional Cliff's d (under minus over).  Primary support
  is counted only from max(abs(d)) across the two features, threshold >0.2.

Audit and resume contract:
- graph source, caches, both OOF archives, source audit, and imported source
  modules are SHA-256-bound.  Missing recorded source hashes refuse execution.
- node order and original IDs are checked against the reloaded graph.  OOF
  archives require exactly ten finite, distinct seed keys.
- NPZ and JSON writes are atomic; completed outputs must match their recorded
  digest and full identity before resuming or entering a summary.
