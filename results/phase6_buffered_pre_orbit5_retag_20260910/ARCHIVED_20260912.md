# Archived buffered CV corpus and structural Moran correlogram (pre orbit-5 retag)

Archived 2026-09-12 by Claude Opus 5 (Task 6 root integration, run C4).

Contents: the complete buffered spatial-CV corpus of 2026-09-10 (20,000 logical fold rows,
13,972 canonical fits, 800 checkpoints, 5 OOF archives, 480 descriptive contrasts; `Buffered
follow-up complete` at 2026-09-10T17:12:11 in `../phase6_buffered_controller_20260910.log`)
and the structural rank-residual Moran correlogram (5,600 rows) that supplied its measured
buffers.

Both were produced against the feature registries and r=1 OOF caches **before** Task 6
finding P1-01 retagged six 5-node orbits (56, 57, 65, 66, 68, 70) from hop 1 to hop 2 and
the affected sweep cells were refit on 2026-09-11. The buffered provenance and preflight
bind `cache_registry_*.csv` hashes and the Moran provenance binds the OOF caches, so
`probe_buffered_cv.py` would refuse to resume, by design. Nothing here was edited.

Known property of this archive (Task 6 finding P3-08): in 15 of the 80 cells the measured
buffer resolved to 1, so 45 of the 330 scored contrasts are aliases of b=1 rows; this
`phase6_buffered_cv_analysis.csv` predates the `is_alias` / `alias_of_pair` columns.
The Moran provenance here also still keys `producer_source_sha256` by absolute local path
(fixed in the producer on 2026-09-11). The fresh run lives at the original paths under
`results/` and supersedes this directory.
