READING
Every one of the 17 pre-retag stars on add_remaining_g5_orbits disappears once the six
hop-2 orbits are removed from the r=1 set. The largest post-retag |mean| on that rung is
0.00054 tau, against pre-retag gains up to
0.03515. The pre-retag "5-node orbits
beat 4-node at r=1" effect was therefore carried entirely by orbits that reach two hops,
i.e. it was a radius effect mislabelled as a graphlet-size effect. This is the same
failure class as the Finding 10 "+0.148 edge tier" (tier tags are inputs, not labels).
The two add_nonorbit_subgraph betweenness flags (email-Eu-core, facebook_combined) are
unchanged, as they must be: those columns did not move.

What this does NOT say: nothing about r >= 2, where the six orbits now live; the
genuine hop-2 contribution of those orbits is a question for the main radius ladder,
not this ablation. Stars remain descriptive (no null, no multiplicity correction).
